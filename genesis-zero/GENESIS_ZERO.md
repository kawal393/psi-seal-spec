## THE METHOD

1. Canonicalisation — RFC 8785 (JSON Canonicalization Scheme). Byte-identical across every language and machine.
2. Digest — SHA-256. Deterministic. Anyone on Earth can recompute it.
3. Signature — Ed25519, plus post-quantum layer (LMS-W4-SHA256, NIST SP 800-208; ML-DSA-65 in the hybrid stack).
4. Anchor — Bitcoin chain tip via OpenTimestamps. The block number is the clock nobody controls.
5. Receipt — public entry in the APEX PSI ledger. Verifiable by anyone at any time.
